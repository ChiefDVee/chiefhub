Why these exist

Everyone I know — me included — was going mad from the ads. Pop-ups, banners, "watch this video to continue", "create an account first". Just to roll some dice or play a game of cards.

So I built my own. Some started as games for my daughter, some as tools I needed myself. I put them online because sooner or later everybody runs into ad rage, so you might as well use them too.

No ads. No signup. No trackers. No personal data collected — there's nothing here worth harvesting anyway. Free, and they stay free. That's the whole business model.
